Fall of the Rift

Date
1487 AS (about five weeks of siege)

The Rift
Attacker
Baron Mihail, with the Hundred-Eleven under Elric

Lord Andrej, with three times the men and half the weapons
Result
The fortress falls for the first time in about 300 years

In 1487 AS Baron Mihail took the fortress that was said to be untakeable. He did it with trebuchets, with his own axe, and with a company of bound men already inside the walls.

Forces

Lord Andrej had about three times Mihail's numbers but only half the weapons. He also had at least two mages: a fire-mage, whose living, molten fire climbed walls and ate through skin, and a mind-witch, whose ghosts felt like icy fingers inside the skull. Mihail had trebuchets, rams, cavalry and the Hundred-Eleven.

Course of the siege

  • Approach. In skirmishes in the forests before the siege, the mages killed several of the company and burned Dean Malkiron's face.
  • Infiltration. Five squads crossed the Olta while the rest made a diversion. Dean Kellen took a group through the sewer tunnel into the city, carrying only spearheads.
  • Terror. Inside, on the book's orders, the infiltrators killed civilians, at first one a day each, to spread fear. The scout Zorimar was caught and flayed alive in the market. The 111 mark on his spearhead exposed the plot.
  • Hunger. Bodies in the wells and fights over mouldy bread. The Sixth squad was trapped and killed in the merchant district; Dean Zane alone got out.
  • Breach. Mihail rode through the broken gate on his red-eyed horse, his axe smoking and spells sliding off him. The fighting went from street to street.
  • End. Lord Andrej was taken at the breach, stripped, and left in a cage in the sun without water until he died.

Aftermath

The company had lost so many men that, once the Grimoire was reopened, its youngest soldiers' brands dropped by about thirty numbers. The council in Ocristan still refused Mihail's claim to the throne, which led to his journey south to Beit Haifud. A local girl named Mira was murdered during the siege, almost certainly by one of the company. Her death appears in no record but one.

Loose threads

  • Andrej was betrayed from inside. Olegzander is the likeliest traitor, but what was he paid, and by whom?
  • Where did Andrej's two mages come from, and does anyone still have them on the payroll?
  • The fortress had held for 300 years. Did its fall change anything in the stone?
