The Silence is the disappearance of the people who quarried and shaped kimberstone. Most of the continent counts its years from it. A date written 1488 AS means the 1,488th year after the Silence.

The builders

Almost nothing is known about the builders except what they left. Kimberstone is white, does not weather, and cannot be worked by anyone now living. Their work includes the fortress-city of the Rift and the bridge legs standing in the Vorn Danu. No writing in their hand has ever been read.

The name

The oldest surviving annals start after a blank. Their first entries describe a season when no bell, bird or voice carried, and they begin counting from the end of that season. Whether the blank covers a year or a century is not known. The calendar puts it at zero for convenience.

Theories

Every theory of the Silence is also a theory of the Thirteen:

  • The Thirteen caused it.
  • The Thirteen are what was left of the builders after it.
  • The Thirteen came afterward, into an empty world.

There are older beliefs too: that the builders went down rather than away, and that kimberstone is less a building material than a lid.
