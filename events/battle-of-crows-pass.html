Battle of Crow's Pass

1472 AS

Crow's Pass. Location unrecorded, most likely in the northern range.
Notable dead

Three children

Crow's Pass is remembered for one death: Baron Mihail's. Who fought whom, and who won, matters much less to the story people tell.

The death

Mihail took three arrows in the chest during the fighting and died. He was carried off the field by his own men.

The bargain

The commonly told version is that his family hired a witch to bring him back, and that she named her price after the work was done: his three youngest children. Mihail rose. The children were not seen again.

The bargain is not recorded anywhere, but there is evidence for it. Since 1472 AS Mihail has not been seen to eat or to age, his skin has gone waxen, and his eyes shine gold in the dark. People who knew him before say he never used to be cold.

Loose threads

  • Where is Crow's Pass, and is anything still buried there?
  • Who was the witch? Answered in part: a woman of the Blackmire line who came north for the work. Whether the line answers to anything larger, perhaps one of the Thirteen, is unknown.
  • Were the three children killed, or taken somewhere and kept? Narrowed: three stories are told, none proven. See The Blackmire.
