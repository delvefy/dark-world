Andrej was the last lord of the Rift and a rival baron to Mihail. He was a careful, capable commander. He held the fortress for five weeks against a larger army and lost it to an enemy that was already inside his walls. His captors left him to die of thirst in a cage.

The house

Andrej's house held the Rift for generations. Its records were kept in the fortress and burned in the sack, so how long the house held it, and whether the house took it in the capture of c. 1187 AS, cannot now be checked.

The house did something no other Veleh house is known to do: it trained its own mages. Veleh has no mage corps. The empires do, and they do not lend their mages to small lords. Andrej's house raised its mages in the household from childhood and kept them as kin. At the siege of 1487 AS it had two:

  • The fire-mage. Andrej's half-sister, a middle-aged woman with grey-streaked hair. Her fire ran like molten metal, but alive: it stuck to whatever it touched, climbed walls and ate through skin. In the forest skirmishes before the siege she wiped out half a squad of the Hundred-Eleven and burned Dean Malkiron's face. She was still fighting at the breach, and died in the street fighting that followed.
  • The mind-witch. A woman of the household who had been nurse to Andrej's children. Her power showed as ghostly figures and a feeling of icy fingers raking the inside of the skull. Dean Malkiron cut her throat in an ambush in the forests, and the spell ended at once.

Both died in 1487 AS. Whatever the house knew about raising mages was not written anywhere that survived.

The siege

Mihail came to the Rift with the larger army: his own levies and cavalry, trebuchets and rams, and the Hundred-Eleven. Andrej's garrison was smaller, about three times the size of the company, and had half the weapons. Against that he had the Rift itself, which had made small garrisons beat large armies for three hundred years. He had the rivers on two sides and two bridges that could be held by a few men.

Andrej fought well. His mages hurt the company before the siege began. He held the walls for five weeks under bombardment, and he caught and flayed one of the company's scouts in his own market.

He lost because the company was inside. Five squads crossed the Olta, and a party under Dean Kellen came in through the sewer tunnel. Bound to the Grimoire, they could not be broken under torture. They killed civilians one a day each, put bodies in the wells, and turned the city's hunger into panic. When Mihail's army made its assault, men of the company helped open the gate from the inside. Mihail rode through it on his red-eyed horse, and the fighting went street by street.

Nobody betrayed Andrej. The gate was opened from within, but by the company, not by his own men.

The cage

Andrej was taken at the breach. He was stripped and put in a cage in the sun without water, and he took several days to die. Mihail is not recorded as visiting him. What became of his children is not recorded either.

The lie

The story that brought down King Olegzander said Andrej had been sold from inside, and it named the hour and the gate. That part was close to the truth: a gate was opened from inside, near enough to the hour named. The story was false only about who opened it. Whoever wrote it knew how the Rift fell, and knew it early.

Loose threads

  • The Rift guarded Ustrus's tax road, and Ustrus sent no relief in five weeks. Was Andrej left to fall? Narrowed: the decision lay with the Counting-House, and its reasons are unknown.
  • His children's nurse was a mind-witch. Where are the children, and did they learn anything from her?
  • Who taught Andrej's house to raise mages, with no corps and no school in Veleh?
