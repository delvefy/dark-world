Grigore Tal is the castellan of the Rift, holding the fortress in the name of Lord Andrej's young son for the boy's guardian, Baron Mihail. He is one of the household men who carried Mihail off the field at Crow's Pass in 1472 AS and kept the pass for sixteen years afterward. He was the first of that garrison to be relieved. He has never said what he saw under the arch, and he will not stand in a gateway.

The pass

In 1472 AS Tal was a sergeant of Mihail's levies. He was beside him at the summit when the Blorian arrows struck and was one of the four men who carried the body back under the arch. He stayed through the night that followed, when the witch of the Blackmire line had the body laid across the sill. See Battle of Crow's Pass.

Afterward he was one of the twenty or so men who stayed at the pass as its garrison. For sixteen years they kept travellers away from the sill and were not relieved. When Mihail took the Rift in 1487 AS and needed a castellan he could trust, he sent to the pass for Tal. The pass garrison has had one fewer man since.

Tal has not been back. He has been heard to say that he does not need to go: he can still see it.

The arch

Tal is the only man of the pass garrison who has lived among other people since 1472 AS, and so the only one anyone has been able to question. He answers every question about the night at the arch the same way, with one word: no. He does not say he does not remember. He has never been drunk enough to say more, and the men under him say he does not drink.

What he does instead has been noticed. He will not stand in a gateway or under an arch, and he does not sit with his back to a door. He gives his orders at the Rift's gates from beside them, not under them, and he crosses the bridges at a walk with his eyes on the far end. His quarters in the citadel are an inner room with no window. He sleeps with a lamp lit.

The men of the old Rift garrison have made a story of it. They say he saw something come through the arch, and that it has not finished coming.

The garrison

The Rift's garrison in 1488 AS is two bodies of men under one officer. Mihail's men hold the gates, the bridges and the citadel. The surviving soldiers of Lord Andrej's garrison serve in the walls and the towers. They swore to the boy Vasko, as their lord's heir, under the wardship arrangement, and Tal holds them to that oath strictly. He keeps the two bodies in separate quarters and separate watches. There has been no killing between them that has been reported.

When what was left of the Hundred-Eleven came back from Trantar in 1488 AS, Tal had the gate opened without asking who they were. He already knew. The men of Andrej's garrison watched the company ride in under the walls it had opened from inside a year before. They said nothing either.

The girl

In 1488 AS it was proposed in Mihail's household that Andrej's children be lodged at the Rift, where the boy's lordship lay. The men of Andrej's old garrison sent Tal a petition. They would serve the boy. They asked that the girl, Ilinca, not be brought into the fortress.

They had fought beside the nurse who raised her. They remembered what her gift did to the company in the forests: the figures that were not there, the cold fingers inside the skull. They had been told, as soldiers are told such things, that the nurse had been teaching the girl since infancy. Several of them said plainly that they would not sleep under the same roof as her.

Tal sent the petition to Mihail without comment. The children have not been lodged at the Rift. Whether that was the petition's doing is unknown.

The tax road

The Rift guards the road the Counting-House's riders used for fifty years. In the spring of 1488 AS Mihail offered Mechil to close it. It has not been closed. Riders in Ustrus livery still cross the Rift's bridges with their leather boxes. Tal has them counted in and out at each bridge, by name where they will give one and by description where they will not, and the counts are sent to Ocristan. What Mihail does with them is not known. See The Counting-House.
