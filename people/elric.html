Elric commands the Hundred-Eleven. He keeps the Grimoire and is the only one who opens it. He has outlived several full companies.

Origin

Elric came from the far side of the Eastern Sea. He describes it as a wild country of beasts and dark mages. He crossed around 1424 AS: twenty-one days east to west, nine of them in fever, tied to the mast. Asked what he was running from, he has said only that whatever it was is still out there.

That puts his crossing at least sixty-three years before 1487 AS. His real age is unknown. He looks old and weathered, not ancient. The binding probably accounts for the difference.

Character

He speaks low and evenly and never needs to raise his voice. He is practical to the point of cruelty. He will leave his own men behind if the contract requires it, and he threatens discipline in precise, ugly terms. He is loyal to the contract he has signed and to no living person.

He treats the Grimoire less as a master than as a long-time partner. He decides who receives its healing and when it is opened to rewrite the brands. At the Rift in 1488 AS he read names from it for an hour.

Record

  • 1487 AS. Leads the company in the fall of the Rift. Escorts Mihail to Beit Haifud and brings the gold north by land and sea. Directs the killings in Ocristan.
  • 1488 AS. Holds Trantar and loses it. Fights his way east through the woods with eleven men and rejoins the survivors near the Rift.

Loose threads

  • What is still out there, beyond the Eastern Sea?
  • Did Elric find the Grimoire, inherit it, or was he found by it?
  • Does he know about the Thirteen? He spoke the southern tongue at the Ustrus court as if he had been there before.
