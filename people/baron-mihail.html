Mihail is a Veleh baron who has been bidding for the throne by force since the king's death in 1484 AS: first against Olegzander, crowned by Ustrus's council, then for the empty seat he left. It is widely believed that he died fifteen years earlier and was brought back.

Appearance

His skin is pale as candle wax, and in low light his eyes shine a wolfish gold. No one has seen him eat, and he does not seem to age. He rides a black stallion whose eyes glow red. Spells slide off his armour. His battle-axe trails dark smoke, and at the breach of the Rift it killed three or four defenders a swing. When he comes into a room it gets colder. He never raises his voice.

The revenant rumour

At Crow's Pass in 1472 AS Mihail took three arrows in the chest and died. The story is that his family paid a witch of the Blackmire to bring him back, and that her price was his three youngest children. Nobody has disproved it, and Mihail has never denied it.

Caught between three empires

Each of the three empires around Veleh has one of the Thirteen behind it. Mihail takes money from two of them and is watched by the third:

  • Bloria funds him as a proxy against Ustrus.
  • Ustrus, through its Emperor Tamarun, gave him a chest of gold in Beit Haifud in 1487 AS. He then used it to remove Ustrus's own man in Ocristan, Voievod Olegzander.
  • Mechil has made no move. In 1488 AS Mihail went east to court it; see The Mechil Embassy.

Someone also paid to stop him reaching Beit Haifud: bloodhounds along the road, then two unflagged galleys at sea. The evidence points back into Ustrus itself. See The Gold Run.

Record

  • 1472 AS. Dies at Crow's Pass. Returns.
  • 1487 AS. Takes the Rift. The council rejects his claim. He goes to Beit Haifud for gold, buys the council and has the refusers killed. Olegzander's chair is left empty.
  • 1488 AS. Absent from Trantar, said to be east. In fact meeting envoys of Mechil; see The Mechil Embassy.

Loose threads

  • Who was the witch of Crow's Pass, and does she still hold a claim on him? Answered in part: a witch of the Blackmire line, name unknown. Blackmire debts do not end, so the claim most likely stands.
  • Why did Ustrus pay the man who would depose its own proxy? Narrowed: it was a loan, with terms not yet public. See The Gold Run.
  • What was Mihail doing in the east while Trantar burned? Answered: offering Mechil a default on the Ustrus loan. See The Mechil Embassy.
  • During the fight at sea, the water around him went flat in a ring while the rest of the sea rolled. What else does he carry back from the dead?
