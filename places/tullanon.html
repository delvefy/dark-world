Tullanon is the main town of Olta, in western Veleh. Half of it stands on rotting wooden pillars over the swamps along the Olta River. Its slums lean over the water at bad angles.

The town

The swamps around Tullanon are thick with clams, and clams are its trade. Fish guts and standing water make the smell. Most mornings, fog lies over the ground up to the ankle.

The town is built in two layers: stone and timber on the few dry rises, pillar-houses everywhere else. Pillars rot from the waterline up, so the poorer quarters are always half-sunk and half-rebuilt. The street poor sleep under the boardwalks in summer and freeze on top of them in winter. The worst of the cold is called the long night.

History

In 1487 AS the Hundred-Eleven pitched a recruiting tent in Tullanon. The bait was a pot of stew. A street boy named Sadim swore the oath on the Grimoire there, took the brand 111, and renamed himself Alden. The company marched east toward the Rift soon after.

Later that year a column of twenty Olta riders came to Ocristan in support of Baron Mihail. That suggests the Baron had backers among Tullanon's harder men well before his claim was public.

Surroundings

East of the town the Olta runs out of the swamps through flat corn country and ambush forest, toward its meeting with the Krisen at the Rift. The road west goes on to Trantar on the border.

Loose threads

  • Who in Tullanon's clam guilds paid for the Olta riders who joined Mihail?
  • The pillars under the oldest quarter have never rotted. Locals won't say what they're made of.
  • Why did the Hundred-Eleven recruit here, so far west of the war?
