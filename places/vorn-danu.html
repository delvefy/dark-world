Place · River and crossing · Veleh / Ustrus

Vorn Danu

Divides
Veleh (north) from Ustrus (south)
Width
About a kilometre at the crossing
Mouth
A delta on the Eastern Sea, north of Strova
Tributaries
Olta (with the Krisen), the Trantar water
Crossing
Timber bridge on kimberstone legs

The Vorn Danu is the great river of the region and the border between Veleh and Ustrus. It runs west to east along Veleh's southern edge, bends north near the coast, and reaches the Eastern Sea through a delta. The strip of land between that last bend and the sea is Strova.

The river

The water is deep blue-green and fast, with white foam over the rocks. Armies don't swim it and few boatmen try it in spring. The Olta, which has already taken in the Krisen below the Rift, joins it from the north.

The crossing

The main road south from Ocristan crosses on a long timber bridge. The bridge sits on stumps of kimberstone that rise out of the current. They are the legs of a much older bridge, built before the Silence and broken in a war nobody remembers. The timber deck has been rebuilt many times. The legs have never needed it.

In 1487 AS Baron Mihail crossed here on his way south to Beit Haifud, escorted by the Hundred-Eleven. Red-furred bloodhounds attacked the column soon after it reached the Ustrus side. See The Gold Run.

Loose threads

  • The builders' bridge must have had an end on each bank. What stood at its southern end? Answered: a freestanding kimberstone arch like the Crow Gate, now walled inside an Ustrus post. See Breaking of the Bridge.
  • Ferrymen say the river runs flat and silent for one night every year, at the same place. Narrowed: at the crossing, on the night the bridge was broken and the town of Ostrel spent to break it. What the stillness is remains unknown. See Breaking of the Bridge.
  • Who held the bloodhounds' leashes on the southern bank? Narrowed: someone inside Ustrus, working against the Emperor's own bargain. See The Gold Run.
