The Rift is a fortress city cut from kimberstone, the white stone of the lost builders. It stands on the tongue of land where the Olta and the Krisen meet, so the rivers serve as moats on two sides.

Structure

Two broad kimberstone bridges join the fortress to the mainland. Inside the walls the streets are narrow and winding. There are marble halls, and temples with thin needle-towers. A sewer tunnel runs under the walls; in 1487 AS it was used to get men inside.

Nobody alive can quarry, shape or repair kimberstone. Later builders could only add timber and ordinary stone on top of the builders' work. The difference between the two is visible from a distance.

The seventh tower

Centuries ago a wizard named Aran burned his own soul to break the seventh tower. The tower snapped in half, but the fortress did not fall. The broken tower has been left as he left it. He was sent to open a breach for an army that never came. Who sent him is not remembered.

History

  • Before 0 AS. Built by the kimberstone builders.
  • c. 1100 AS. Aran breaks the seventh tower. No assault follows.
  • c. 1187 AS. The Rift's last known capture before 1487. From then on it was said the fortress could not fall.
  • 1487 AS. Besieged and taken by Baron Mihail with the Hundred-Eleven. See Fall of the Rift.
  • 1488 AS. The Hundred-Eleven fell back here after the defeat at Trantar, down to 39 men. The garrison let them in without asking who they were.
