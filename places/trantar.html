Trantar is a walled town on Veleh's western border, well west of Tullanon. A river runs through it under two bridges. A tannery on the south side sets the smell whenever the wind turns.

The rising of 1488 AS

In 1487 AS the Hundred-Eleven broke into squads and went into the western valleys. Their cover was deserters from the eastern wars. Their task was to turn peasants against the tax riders, in villages such as Haldevin. By spring of 1488 AS about four hundred villagers had come with them to Trantar, some with spears and short-swords that had been smuggled in under grain.

Elric held the town. Baron Mihail did not come; he was said to be east, and was in fact meeting envoys of Mechil.

An Ustrus army came up from the south-west with at least three mages in its line. The battle lasted three days:

  • Day one. The company held the centre, the peasants the wings.
  • Day two. Mage-fire destroyed the right wing. Dean Hessen died.
  • Day three. The peasants did not form up. The centre was driven back into the town, and the fighting went street by street.

The company escaped through the eastern gate and burned the granary behind it so the Ustrus army could not winter on it. The retreat east took weeks. By the time the survivors reached the Rift, the brands of the Grimoire counted 39 men.

Loose threads

  • A flock of crows sat on the western tower and did not move when the company rode in. Crows at Trantar, crows at Crow's Pass. Narrowed: north-eastern Veleh holds that Mechil reads crows; a mute brother of the Rookery was seen at Trantar days before the rising. Unconfirmed.
  • What happened to the villages that sent their men? The tax riders will come back. Answered for one: Haldevin was burned and its register taken. The others are unknown.
  • Was the rising meant to win, or meant to fail where Ustrus would see it? Narrowed: it kept Ustrus looking west while Mihail went east. Whether that was the plan is disputed. See The Mechil Embassy.
