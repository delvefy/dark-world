Ocristan is the walled capital of Veleh. It stands in the central heartland, between Olta in the west and Strova on the coast. With the throne empty again since 1487 AS, the council of barons that sits here is the closest thing Veleh has to a government.

Politics

For about fifty years Ustrus has taxed Veleh through politicians placed in Ocristan. In 1484 AS the last king was killed by an arrow through the throat. Most people call it murder. A dozen barons raised levies. In 1485 AS the council, at Ustrus's word, crowned the voievod who ran its tax press.

By 1487 AS two men mattered. King Olegzander, called the Putrid, was Ustrus's man. Baron Mihail was bidding for his crown by force. When the Rift fell, a story that Olegzander had sold it cost him the crown. The story was false.

The buying of the council, 1487 AS

The council rejected Mihail's claim after he took the Rift. Mihail answered by going to Beit Haifud, the Ustrus capital, and came back with gold. With Elric's Hundred-Eleven as his hands, he spent it in Ocristan:

  • Nobles who took the money were walked out through a side gate to men waiting there.
  • Those who refused were killed in their beds, some with their households. One was the merchant house of Karavin, which had funded Olegzander's levies.
  • The council voted again. Afterward Olegzander's chair was empty. Where he went is not recorded.

The gold was Ustrus gold, yet it was spent to remove Ustrus's own man. Why Ustrus allowed this is one of the open questions of the period. See The Thirteen.

Loose threads

  • Ustrus lent the gold. Its creditors will come to Ocristan to collect, and to have Mihail's new position acknowledged. The repayment is not in gold; see The Gold Run.
  • Where is Olegzander? Answered in part: dead within weeks of the vote, by most accounts, near the west gate. Who killed him is not recorded. See Olegzander.
  • A gray-cloaked rider met Elric at the barracks and left by the west gate. Who sent him?
