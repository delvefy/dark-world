Ocristan is the walled capital of Veleh. It stands in the central heartland, between Olta in the west and Strova on the coast. With no king since 1484 AS, the council of barons that sits here is the closest thing Veleh has to a government.

Politics

For about fifty years Ustrus has taxed Veleh through politicians placed in Ocristan. In 1484 AS the last king was killed by an arrow through the throat. Most people call it murder. A dozen barons raised levies, and the council has been weighing their claims since.

Two power-brokers stood out in 1487 AS. Voievod Olegzander was Ustrus's man and the master of the tax press in the countryside. Baron Mihail was bidding for the throne by force.

The buying of the council, 1487 AS

The council rejected Mihail's claim after he took the Rift. Mihail answered by going to Beit Haifud, the Ustrus capital, and came back with gold. With Elric's Hundred-Eleven as his hands, he spent it in Ocristan:

  • Nobles who took the money were walked out through a side gate to men waiting there.
  • Those who refused were killed in their beds, some with their households. One was the merchant house of Karavin, which had funded Olegzander's levies.
  • The council voted again. Afterward Olegzander's chair was empty. Where he went is not recorded.

The gold was Ustrus gold, yet it was spent to remove Ustrus's own man. Why Ustrus allowed this is one of the open questions of the period. See The Thirteen.

Loose threads

  • Ustrus lent the gold. Its creditors will come to Ocristan to collect, and to have Mihail's new position acknowledged.
  • Where is Olegzander? Dead, fled south, or kept somewhere useful?
  • A gray-cloaked rider met Elric at the barracks and left by the west gate. Who sent him?
