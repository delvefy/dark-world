The Ostvald houses are the banking families of Bloria. They lend to the crown, to its lords and, through the lords, to everyone who lives on a lord's land. Their loans are written so that they cannot be repaid in full, and they are secured on people. Where Ustrus takes its subjects by tax, Bloria takes them by debt. Every bill the houses issue is sealed with the blood of someone in bond.

The houses

The houses take their name from the Ostvald, a street of tall narrow counting-halls in the Blorian capital where the exchange has stood for centuries. Five families keep halls there in 1488 AS. Two others failed within living memory. Their debts were not forgiven; they were bought by the remaining five, and the members of the failed families went into bond with the rest.

The houses lend to the crown first. The crown has borrowed from them for its wars against Ustrus for generations and owes more than it could pay in a century. They also lend to lords, against their estates, and lords pledge their tenants against the interest. Few lords in Bloria are free of an Ostvald note.

The bond

An Ostvald loan does not foreclose on land. When a borrower cannot pay, the house takes people: the borrower himself if he is small, his tenants if he is a lord, whole villages if he is a great one. The people taken are said to be in bond. They are held in the debtors' prisons the houses keep in every large town of Bloria, called the Bonds.

Bonded debtors are bled. Their blood goes into the red wax that seals each Ostvald bill, one debtor to one seal, with the debtor's name written on the back. A bill can be cashed only by the person it names; in anyone else's hand the seal is said to blister the skin, and a forged one to rot. When the bill is paid out, the debtor is supposed to be released. Large bills are often held for years before they are cashed.

A debtor whose bill has not been cashed after seven years is moved to what the Bonds call the lower bond. Those prisoners leave by night in closed wagons, under Ostvald escort, on roads that go north. They are taken to Kalderhof, a walled crown estate on the northern moors. What is done to them there is unknown. None has come back.

A harvest

On paper the houses are lenders who want their money. Their conduct does not fit that.

  • Ostvald rates are set so that interest outruns any harvest. The houses rarely accept full repayment when it is offered, and they have refused it from lords who could pay.
  • They lend most freely where people are thickest: river towns, mill country, the crown's own wars.
  • The Blorian countryside is quieter than Veleh's and noticeably emptier. Travellers remark on villages with sound roofs and nobody under them.
  • The houses have never been known to forgive a debt, sell one back, or let a family out of bond once it is in.

The common reading in Bloria, said quietly, is that the houses are not in the business of money. Money is what they spend. What they gather is people, and the lower bond is where the gathering ends.

Who they serve

The houses call themselves the crown's bankers, and the crown is so far in their debt that it cannot refuse them anything. Who sets Ostvald policy is unknown. No family head has ever been seen to make a decision the others had not already agreed.

Many assume the houses serve whichever of the Thirteen stands behind Bloria, the one Blorians call the Lady of the Ledger, as the Counting-House is held to serve Ustrus's. This is unconfirmed. If it is so, the two patrons want the same thing from their subjects, and take it by different roads. What either wants people for is unknown.

In Veleh

Bloria funds Baron Mihail in Ostvald bills, countersigned by its factor in Ocristan, Edrun Vasselt. Every bill Mihail has spent carries a debtor's name. Every sum he draws slowly is a debtor kept longer in the Bonds. Mihail is known to draw slowly.

Some of the names on the backs of his bills are not Blorian. A few are names from the Veleh side of the northern range, from villages whose lords borrowed in Bloria before 1472 AS. How far the houses' notes already reach into Veleh is unknown.
