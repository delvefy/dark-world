The Thirteen

Number
Thirteen, fixed by prophecy
Nature
Nigh-immortal; method of death unknown
Origin
Unknown. Disputed.
Identified
Fewer than thirteen

The Last Dawn
Holdings (1488 AS)
Ustrus, Bloria, Mechil: one each

The Thirteen are thirteen nigh-immortal beings who have shaped the continent from behind thrones for as long as there have been annals. They do not rule openly. They rule through emperors, viziers, banks, churches and debts. Their main enemy is each other.

What is known

  • There are thirteen. The prophecy says so, and nobody has counted more.
  • Not all of them have been identified. Some may sit in places no one suspects. Some may be asleep.
  • None is known to have died for good.
  • They war on one another by proxy. Empires are their pieces, and small kingdoms are where they fight.
  • They want relics, especially ones that bind, such as the Grimoire.

Origin

Nobody knows where they came from, and the question is tied to the Silence. Three positions are held:

  • The breakers. The Thirteen caused the Silence and ended the kimberstone builders.
  • The remnant. The Thirteen are builders, or what survived of them, changed by whatever they did to live through it.
  • The latecomers. The Thirteen came after and took a world the builders had left empty.

No position has been proven. Any source that claims certainty should be treated with suspicion.

The seam of empires

In 1488 AS each of the three empires around Veleh has one of the Thirteen behind it:

  • Ustrus, to the south-east. Its young Emperor, Tamarun, sits the throne in Beit Haifud. Whether he knows what stands behind him is unconfirmed. Its revenue office, the Counting-House, is widely held to answer to that power, not to him.
  • Bloria, to the north-west. It bankrolls Baron Mihail. Blorians call the power behind their crown the Lady of the Ledger: a woman at court who has held the crown's book at every coronation in the rolls.
  • Mechil, to the north-east. It has not moved on Veleh in a hundred years, and few believe it is idle. In 1488 AS it heard Mihail out and did not answer; see The Mechil Embassy.

Veleh is the board between them. Mihail, a man who has already died once, is being paid by two of them and watched by the third.

The Last Dawn

The prophecy (see The Last Dawn) says the Last Dawn will return and destroy the Thirteen. The word return implies the Last Dawn was here before. Who it was, and when, is not recorded.

The name is read two ways. Some take it as a promise: the last night before morning. Others take it as a warning: the last morning there will be. In Veleh, on the worst night of the long night, some households leave a candle burning in an east-facing window. Tax riders have been known to put it out.

Loose threads

  • Which of the Thirteen sent the bloodhounds and the galleys against Mihail? Narrowed: the trail leads back into Ustrus, either its counting-house or the one behind its throne. See The Gold Run.
  • The Grimoire kills any mage who touches it. What would it do to one of the Thirteen?
  • Is anyone in Veleh looking for the Last Dawn? Is anyone hunting for it in order to kill it first? Narrowed: no one openly. See The Last Dawn.
